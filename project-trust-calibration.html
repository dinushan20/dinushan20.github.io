Trust Calibration with Human-in-the-Loop Multi-Objective Bayesian Optimization

Trust Calibration HITL-MOBO

This project investigates how adaptive augmented-visualization feedback may support trust calibration in automated driving. The participant study and data collection have been completed. Analysis, interpretation, and preparation of research outputs are ongoing.

To protect unpublished research, this page currently presents only a high-level overview. Detailed study design, implementation decisions, analyses, and findings will be added when the work is publicly available.

My Technical Contribution

I developed and maintained the Unity-based experimental platform used to conduct the study. My contribution included translating study requirements into an interactive system, supporting the adaptive visualization workflow, implementing structured participant and questionnaire flows, preparing experimental materials, and supporting study execution and data collection.
