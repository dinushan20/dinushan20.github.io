Research project · Data collection complete

Trust Calibration with Human-in-the-Loop Multi-Objective Bayesian Optimization

This study looks at whether adaptive augmented visualisations can help people make their trust in automated driving better match the system’s behaviour. I built and supported the Unity study platform. Data collection is complete, and the analysis is ongoing.

AI-generated illustration for the cognitive-load study.

University research project · Completed

Assessing Cognitive Load in Immersive Environments

We compared cognitive load during 1-back and 3-back tasks across desktop and VR setups using SMEQ ratings, eye tracking, and pupillometry. I worked on the literature review, participant sessions, and data-processing pipeline.

This was an early Unity prototype for exploring reliability scenarios and visual feedback in automated driving. It reached the prototype and study-design stage, but this version was not used for a participant study.
